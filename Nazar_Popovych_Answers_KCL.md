## Question 1 

The thing is that the currents going in the node are positive, and those going out are negative. For that reason, the sum of all the currents going in is always equal to the magnitude of the sum of the currents going out, which results in the sum always becoming balanced to 0. 
"The book says that $-i_{n}$, is defined to be positive for current flowing out of the node." — that is because as $i_n$ is negative since it is flowing out of the node, it becomes, for instance, -(-2 A) = 2 A, which is positive. 

## Question 2 

$\sum_{n=1}^{M} i_n = - \sum_{n=M+1}^{N} i_n$

$\sum_{n=1}^{M} i_n + \sum_{n=M+1}^{N} i_n = 0$

Using the interval partitioning property of sigma notation, 

$\sum_{n=1}^{M} i_n + \sum_{n=M+1}^{N} i_n = \sum_{n=1}^{N} i_n$ 

$\sum_{n=1}^{N} i_n = 0$, which we have already proved in the previous question.
